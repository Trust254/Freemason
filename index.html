ABOUT FREEMASONRY

A Historic Fraternal Institution

Freemasonry has roots associated with medieval stonemasonry and has developed into a network of independent Masonic jurisdictions and Lodges.

⚒

Heritage

Modern Freemasonry draws on symbols, traditions and ceremonies associated with the stonemasons of the medieval period.

∴

Brotherhood

Lodges provide opportunities for fellowship, friendship, reflection and personal development.

✦

Community Service

Charitable giving, volunteering and support for communities are important activities in many Masonic jurisdictions.

HISTORY

Freemasonry Through Time

Selected historical milestones; dates and institutional histories vary by jurisdiction.

Medieval roots

Traditions of operative stonemasons contributed symbols and practices that later became associated with speculative Freemasonry.

1717 — London

Four London Lodges met on 24 June 1717 and formed the first Grand Lodge, a landmark in the development of organized modern Freemasonry.

1723 — Constitutions

James Anderson's Constitutions of the Free-Masons was published, becoming an influential early Masonic constitutional text.

1813 — United Grand Lodge of England

The two principal English Grand Lodges united to form the United Grand Lodge of England.

Today

Freemasonry operates through numerous independent Grand Lodges, Districts, Provinces and local Lodges around the world.

WORLDWIDE

A Network of Masonic Jurisdictions

There is no single worldwide Grand Lodge controlling every Masonic organization. Recognition and membership rules depend on jurisdiction.

How membership is normally approached

  1. Register interest with the relevant recognized organization.
  2. Identify a suitable local Lodge or Masonic area.
  3. Provide the requested personal details.
  4. A local representative contacts the applicant.
  5. The organization's own membership process determines eligibility and admission.

FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Is this website itself a Grand Lodge?

No. A genuine deployment must be owned and authorized by the specific Masonic organization represented. This distinction protects applicants from impersonation and payment fraud.

Does submitting an application make me a Freemason?

No. An enquiry is only the beginning of the process. Membership is determined by the relevant Masonic jurisdiction and Lodge.

Are there membership fees?

Fees and dues vary between jurisdictions and Lodges. Applicants should obtain the official fee schedule and payment instructions directly from the recognized organization.

Can women join Freemasonry?

It depends on the organization. Some jurisdictions operate male-only Lodges while separate women-only and mixed organizations also exist.
